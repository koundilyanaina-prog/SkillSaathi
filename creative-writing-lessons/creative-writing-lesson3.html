Characters and Motivation

Lesson Overview

Create characters with goals, needs and believable choices.

Key Concepts

  • A character's goal is what they want; motivation explains why it matters.
  • Strengths, flaws and contradictions make characters feel more human.
  • Character change can happen through choices and consequences.

Practical Activity

  1. Create a character profile with a goal, fear and strength.
  2. Write a short scene showing the character making a choice.
  3. Show the character through action rather than a list of traits.
Try it yourself: Complete the activity with your own idea. Use free tools or paper sketches if a particular app is unavailable.

Quick Knowledge Check

What should guide your creative decisions first?

Suggested answer: The purpose and audience of the work.
