Final Short Story Project

Lesson Overview

Complete a short story with a beginning, middle and ending.

Key Concepts

  • A finished story has a coherent character journey and meaningful conflict.
  • Use details, dialogue and pacing to support the mood.
  • Proofread while keeping the writer's individual voice.

Practical Activity

  1. Write a short story of approximately 800–1200 words.
  2. Include a clear protagonist, conflict, turning point and resolution.
  3. Revise the draft and prepare a title and short author note.
Try it yourself: Complete the activity with your own idea. Use free tools or paper sketches if a particular app is unavailable.

Quick Knowledge Check

What should guide your creative decisions first?

Suggested answer: The purpose and audience of the work.
