PRACTICAL PROJECT 2 OF 2

Website Content Pack

Project Brief

Prepare core written content for a small website.

Goal: Apply the skills from this course in a complete piece of creative work.

Project Instructions

  1. Write a concise homepage introduction.
  2. Draft an About section and one service or course description.
  3. Create clear headings and calls to action.
  4. Check consistency of tone and factual details.
  5. Proofread the complete content pack.

Final Checklist

  • The work has a clear purpose and audience.
  • Content is original or appropriately attributed.
  • Layout and presentation are consistent.
  • Spelling, details and exported files have been checked.
  • The final work is saved in an organised folder.
