MICROSOFT WORD · PRACTICAL PROJECT 01

Professional Resume

Learn how to create a clean, organised and professional resume using Microsoft Word. Apply your formatting, paragraph and page layout skills in this practical project.

Project Objectives

  • Understand the structure of a professional resume.
  • Use headings, fonts and paragraph formatting.
  • Organise education, skills and experience.
  • Create a clean and readable page layout.
  • Save and export the final resume as a PDF.

Sample Resume Layout

Use this sample as a reference while creating your own resume in Microsoft Word.

YOUR NAME

City, State | your.email@example.com | +91 00000 00000

LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/yourprofile

CAREER OBJECTIVE

Motivated and organised individual seeking an opportunity to apply my knowledge, develop professional skills and contribute positively to an organisation.

  • Describe your responsibilities and daily tasks.
  • Mention relevant work and achievements.

Write a short description of your project and the tools used.

  • Communication and teamwork
  • Problem-solving
  • Time management

DECLARATION

I hereby declare that the information provided above is true and correct to the best of my knowledge.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open Microsoft Word and select Blank Document.
  2. Type your name and contact information at the top.
  3. Add headings such as Career Objective, Education and Skills.
  4. Use bold formatting for your name and section headings.
  5. Use bullet points for skills and responsibilities.
  6. Adjust margins, spacing and alignment.
  7. Review spelling and grammar.
  8. Save the document as My_Professional_Resume.docx.
  9. Export another copy as PDF.

Formatting Guidelines

  • Keep your resume clean and professional.
  • Use readable fonts such as Arial or Calibri.
  • Use 16–20 pt for your name and 11–12 pt for body text.
  • Keep section headings consistent.
  • Avoid unnecessary colours and decorative elements.
  • Try to keep an entry-level resume to one page when practical.

Your Practical Task

Create your own professional resume in Microsoft Word. Replace all sample information with your own details. Include your education, skills, projects and experience wherever applicable.

Final Submission: Save both DOCX and PDF versions.
