MICROSOFT WORD · PRACTICAL PROJECT 02

School Assignment / Report

Learn to prepare a well-structured academic assignment using headings, paragraphs, page formatting, tables and references.

Project Objectives

  • Create a professional title page.
  • Organise content using headings and subheadings.
  • Format paragraphs, margins and page numbers.
  • Insert tables and images where needed.
  • Create a clean and readable academic report.

Sample Assignment Structure

ENVIRONMENTAL POLLUTION

A School Assignment

Submitted by: Student Name

Class: 10 | Subject: Science

School: Your School Name

1. Introduction

Environmental pollution occurs when harmful substances enter our surroundings and affect air, water or land. It can harm humans, animals and plants.

2. Types of Pollution

Air Pollution: Caused by smoke, vehicles and industrial emissions.

Water Pollution: Caused by waste, chemicals and plastic entering water bodies.

Land Pollution: Caused by littering, chemicals and improper waste disposal.

3. Effects of Pollution

Pollution can cause health problems, damage ecosystems, reduce biodiversity and contribute to climate change.

4. Prevention and Solutions

Plant trees, reduce plastic use, recycle waste, conserve water and use cleaner energy sources.

5. Conclusion

Protecting the environment is a shared responsibility. Small actions by individuals can create positive changes.

6. References

1. Science Textbook

2. School Learning Materials

3. Relevant educational resources

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open Microsoft Word and create a blank document.
  2. Prepare a title page with assignment title and student details.
  3. Add an introduction and divide the content into sections.
  4. Use Heading styles for titles and subheadings.
  5. Adjust margins, font size and line spacing.
  6. Insert a relevant table or image if required.
  7. Add page numbers and references.
  8. Review spelling and grammar.
  9. Save as School_Assignment.docx and export as PDF.

Formatting Guidelines

  • Use Arial or Times New Roman.
  • Use 14–16 pt for main headings and 11–12 pt for body text.
  • Keep paragraph alignment and spacing consistent.
  • Use page numbers on content pages.
  • Keep the title page neat and simple.

Your Practical Task

Create a 3–5 page assignment on a topic of your choice. Include a title page, introduction, at least three main sections, conclusion and references.

Final Submission: DOCX and PDF versions.
