Professional Business Presentations

Lesson Overview

A business presentation should be audience-focused and action-oriented.

Key Concepts

  • Start with the purpose and audience need.
  • Organise the content into problem, insight, recommendation and next steps.
  • Use evidence and charts only when they support the recommendation.
  • End with a clear decision or action requested.

Practical Activity

Open Microsoft PowerPoint and complete these activities:

  1. Draft a short proposal for a simple business idea.
  2. Include problem, solution, benefits and next steps.
  3. Present it in a concise 5-slide deck.
Remember: Save your work regularly. Try the activity yourself instead of only reading the steps.

Quick Knowledge Check

Which approach usually makes a presentation easier to follow?

Correct answer: Use one clear idea per slide with readable text.
