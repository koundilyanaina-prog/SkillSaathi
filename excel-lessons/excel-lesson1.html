Meet Your First Spreadsheet

Welcome to Microsoft Excel! In this lesson, you will understand what Excel is, how spreadsheets work, and how to enter your first data into a worksheet.

  • How cell addresses work.
  • How to enter and edit data.
  • How to save an Excel workbook.

1. What is Microsoft Excel?

Microsoft Excel is a spreadsheet application used to organize, calculate, analyze and present information.

It arranges information in rows and columns. You can enter numbers, text, dates and formulas into individual cells.

Where is Excel used?

  • Creating monthly expense records.
  • Managing student marks and attendance.
  • Preparing salary sheets.
  • Maintaining business records.
  • Creating reports and charts.

2. Workbook and Worksheet

These are two important terms you should understand before working with Excel.

Workbook

A workbook is the complete Excel file. It can contain one or more worksheets.

Worksheet

A worksheet is an individual spreadsheet inside a workbook. It contains rows, columns and cells.

3. Understanding Rows and Columns

An Excel worksheet is made up of rows and columns.

  • Rows: Horizontal lines identified by numbers (1, 2, 3...).
  • Columns: Vertical lines identified by letters (A, B, C...).
  • Cells: Small boxes formed where a row and column meet.
A B C
1 Name Subject Marks
2 Asha Maths 88
3 Ravi Science 92
4 Meena English 81

In this example, A is a column, 1 is a row, and A1 is a cell.

4. What is a Cell Address?

Every cell in Excel has a unique address. It is created by combining the column letter and row number.

A1 means Column A, Row 1.

B2 means Column B, Row 2.

C4 means Column C, Row 4.

The currently selected cell is called the Active Cell.

5. Types of Data in Excel

You can enter different types of information into Excel cells.

Text

Names, cities, subjects and descriptions.

Numbers

Marks, prices, quantities and amounts.

Dates

Dates such as 07/11/2026.

Formulas

Instructions that perform calculations. Most Excel formulas begin with an equals sign (=).

6. Entering and Editing Data

Follow these basic steps:

  1. Open Microsoft Excel.
  2. Select Blank Workbook.
  3. Click any cell.
  4. Type your information.
  5. Press Enter to move to the next row.
  6. Select a cell and type again to replace its content.

7. Saving Your Excel File

Saving your work is important. It helps you keep your data safe and use it later.

Keyboard Shortcut

Ctrl + S — Save your workbook.

Excel workbooks are commonly saved with the .xlsx file extension.
