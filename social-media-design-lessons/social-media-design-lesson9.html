Content Calendar and Brand Consistency

Lesson Overview

Plan a sustainable publishing rhythm.

Key Concepts

  • A calendar records topic, format, caption, asset status and planned date.
  • Reuse design systems while varying the content itself.
  • Review performance in context rather than judging a post by one metric.

Practical Activity

  1. Create a two-week calendar with six planned posts.
  2. Assign a content pillar and format to each post.
  3. Add a simple checklist for design and proofreading.
Try it yourself: Complete the activity with your own idea. Use free tools or paper sketches if a particular app is unavailable.

Quick Knowledge Check

What should guide your creative decisions first?

Suggested answer: The purpose and audience of the work.
