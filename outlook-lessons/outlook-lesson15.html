Creating Meetings and Invitations

Lesson Overview

Meeting invitations help coordinate people and schedules.

Key Concepts

  • A meeting invitation usually includes a title, attendees, date, time and location or online meeting link.
  • Check attendee availability where the account supports it.
  • Include an agenda so participants know the purpose.
  • Update or cancel invitations if plans change.

Practical Activity

  1. Draft a fictional team meeting invitation.
  2. Add a short agenda and expected duration.
  3. Write a polite response accepting or declining an invitation.
Safety reminder: Use practice data only. Never share your password or one-time verification codes.

Quick Knowledge Check

Which is the safest habit when handling an unexpected email attachment?

Correct answer: Verify the sender and context before opening it.
