Lesson 14 of 25 | Inserting Pictures and Shapes

🎯 Learning Objectives

In this lesson, you will learn how to insert and customize pictures and shapes in a Word document.

  • Insert pictures from your computer.
  • Use online pictures where available.
  • Resize, crop and rotate pictures.
  • Understand text wrapping.
  • Insert and format shapes.
  • Arrange images and shapes professionally.

1. Inserting Pictures

Pictures make documents more attractive and help explain information visually.

How to Insert a Picture from Your Computer

  1. Open your Word document.
  2. Go to the Insert tab.
  3. Click Pictures.
  4. Choose This Device or the equivalent option.
  5. Find and select your image.
  6. Click Insert.

Online Pictures

In supported versions, you can use Insert → Pictures → Online Pictures to find images from available online sources.

Use images that you own or have permission to use. Check licensing before publishing or distributing a document.

2. Resizing a Picture

Resizing changes the width and height of an image.

  1. Click the picture.
  2. Small sizing handles will appear around it.
  3. Drag a corner handle to increase or decrease the size.
  4. Use the Picture Format tab for precise height and width values.
Tip: Use a corner handle to maintain the picture's proportions. Avoid stretching images unevenly.

3. Cropping and Rotating Pictures

Crop a Picture

Cropping removes unwanted outer portions of an image.

  1. Select the picture.
  2. Open Picture Format.
  3. Click Crop.
  4. Drag the crop handles.
  5. Press Enter or click outside the image to apply.

Rotate a Picture

  1. Select the picture.
  2. Open Picture Format.
  3. Use Rotate to rotate or flip the image.

4. Text Wrapping

Text wrapping controls how text flows around a picture.

  • In Line with Text: The picture behaves like a text character.
  • Square: Text flows around the rectangular boundary.
  • Tight: Text follows the shape of the picture more closely, where supported.
  • Through: Text can flow through open areas of an image, where supported.
  • Top and Bottom: Text appears above and below the picture.
  • Behind Text: The picture appears behind the text.
  • In Front of Text: The picture appears above the text.

How to Change Text Wrapping

  1. Select the picture.
  2. Click the Layout Options button or open Picture Format.
  3. Choose the required wrapping option.

5. Picture Styles and Effects

Word provides picture formatting options to improve the appearance of images.

  • Picture Styles: Apply ready-made frames and designs.
  • Picture Border: Add an outline around the image.
  • Picture Effects: Add shadow, reflection or glow effects.
  • Corrections: Adjust sharpness, brightness and contrast.
  • Color: Adjust saturation or recolour an image.

6. Inserting Shapes

Shapes are ready-made graphical objects used to highlight information or create diagrams.

Common Shapes

  • Rectangle and square
  • Circle and oval
  • Lines and arrows
  • Triangles
  • Callouts
  • Flowchart shapes

How to Insert a Shape

  1. Go to the Insert tab.
  2. Click Shapes.
  3. Select a shape.
  4. Click and drag on the page to draw it.

7. Formatting Shapes

After selecting a shape, the Shape Format tab provides formatting options.

  • Shape Fill: Changes the inside colour.
  • Shape Outline: Changes the border colour and style.
  • Shape Effects: Adds effects such as shadow or glow.
  • Size: Changes height and width.
  • Rotate: Rotates the shape.

Adding Text to a Shape

  1. Right-click the shape.
  2. Select Add Text or Edit Text, depending on the version.
  3. Type your required text.
  4. Format the text as needed.

8. Arranging Pictures and Shapes

When a document contains multiple objects, you can control how they are positioned.

  • Bring Forward: Moves an object in front of another object.
  • Send Backward: Moves an object behind another object.
  • Align: Aligns objects with each other or the page.
  • Group: Combines selected objects so they can be moved together.
  • Rotate: Changes the direction of an object.
Grouping is useful when you want a picture and its related shapes to move together. Select multiple objects first.

9. Practical Activity

Create a document named Picture-Shape-Practice.docx.

  1. Insert a picture from your computer.
  2. Resize it using a corner handle.
  3. Crop the picture.
  4. Apply a picture border.
  5. Insert a rectangle and a circle.
  6. Change their fill colours.
  7. Add text inside the rectangle.
  8. Arrange the picture and shapes neatly.
  9. Save your document.
