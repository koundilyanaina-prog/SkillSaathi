Lesson 10 of 25 | Page Setup and Margins

🎯 Learning Objectives

In this lesson, you will learn how to prepare the page layout of a Microsoft Word document.

  • Understand page setup.
  • Change paper size.
  • Use portrait and landscape orientation.
  • Adjust page margins.
  • Set custom page dimensions and margins.

1. What is Page Setup?

Page Setup controls the overall layout of a document. It determines how the page looks when you view, print or export it.

Important page setup options include:

  • Margins
  • Orientation
  • Paper Size
  • Page layout settings
Remember: Page setup affects the page layout, while font and paragraph formatting affect the text.

2. Page Orientation

Orientation decides whether a page is vertical or horizontal.

Portrait Orientation

Portrait means the page is taller than it is wide. It is commonly used for letters, resumes and reports.

Landscape Orientation

Landscape means the page is wider than it is tall. It is useful for wide tables, charts and presentations.

How to change orientation:
  1. Open the Layout tab.
  2. Click Orientation.
  3. Select Portrait or Landscape.

3. Paper Size

Paper Size determines the dimensions of the document page.

Paper SizeCommon Use
A4Letters, assignments and office documents
LetterCommonly used in the United States
LegalLegal and longer-format documents

How to Change Paper Size

  1. Open the Layout tab.
  2. Click Size.
  3. Choose the required paper size, such as A4.
For many standard documents in India, A4 is a common choice. Always follow the required format if your school, college or office specifies one.

4. Understanding Page Margins

Margins are the blank spaces around the edges of a page. They keep text from getting too close to the paper edges.

Four Main Margins

  • Top: Space above the content.
  • Bottom: Space below the content.
  • Left: Space on the left side.
  • Right: Space on the right side.

Common Margin Presets

  • Normal
  • Narrow
  • Moderate
  • Wide
  • Mirrored

5. How to Change Margins

  1. Open the Layout tab.
  2. Click Margins.
  3. Choose a preset such as Normal or Narrow.
  4. To enter your own values, click Custom Margins.
  5. Enter the Top, Bottom, Left and Right measurements.
  6. Click OK.
Example: A report may use standard margins, while a document with a wide table may need a different layout.

6. Custom Page Setup

The Page Setup dialog box gives you more control over your document.

  1. Open the Layout tab.
  2. Click the small dialog box launcher in the Page Setup group.
  3. Adjust margins, paper size and orientation.
  4. Review the settings.
  5. Click OK to apply them.

You can also use the Apply to option, where available, to apply settings to the whole document or a selected section.

Tip: Check Print Preview before printing to make sure the page layout looks correct.

7. Practical Activity

Create a document called Page-Setup-Practice.docx and complete these tasks:

  1. Type a short report about your computer learning journey.
  2. Set paper size to A4.
  3. Choose Portrait orientation.
  4. Set Normal margins.
  5. Save the document.
  6. Change orientation to Landscape and observe the difference.
  7. Change it back to Portrait and save again.
