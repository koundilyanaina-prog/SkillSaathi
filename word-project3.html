MICROSOFT WORD · PRACTICAL PROJECT 03

Business Letter and Invoice

Practise creating formal business communication and a structured invoice using Microsoft Word.

Project Objectives

  • Understand the format of a formal business letter.
  • Use professional greetings and closing statements.
  • Create an invoice using Word tables.
  • Format dates, amounts and business information.
  • Prepare documents suitable for professional communication.

Part A: Sample Business Letter

ABC OFFICE SOLUTIONS

123 Main Road, Ranchi, Jharkhand

Email: contact@example.com | Phone: +91 00000 00000

Date: 23 November 2026

To,

The Purchase Manager

XYZ Enterprises

Ranchi, Jharkhand


Subject: Request for Office Stationery Supply


Dear Sir/Madam,

We would like to request a quotation for office stationery items required by our organisation. Kindly share the available products, pricing details and expected delivery schedule.

We would appreciate receiving your response at the earliest convenience. Thank you for your time and cooperation.


Yours sincerely,

Rahul Sharma

Office Administrator

ABC Office Solutions

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open Word and create a new document.
  2. Type the company name and contact details.
  3. Write the date, recipient details and subject.
  4. Write the letter body using formal language.
  5. Add a polite closing and sender information.
  6. Start a new page for the invoice.
  7. Insert a table with item, quantity, rate and amount columns.
  8. Enter subtotal, tax and grand total.
  9. Apply borders, alignment and professional formatting.
  10. Save both documents as DOCX and PDF.

Your Practical Task

Create one business letter for a fictional company and one invoice containing at least four products or services. Use tables and consistent formatting.

Final Submission: Business letter and invoice in DOCX and PDF formats.
