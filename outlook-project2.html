Inbox Organisation Plan

Project Brief

Design a practical system for keeping a busy inbox organised and safe.

Goal: Apply the course skills in realistic but fictional situations.

Project Instructions

  1. Create a folder structure for Study, Work, Applications and Reference.
  2. Write three example rules using clear conditions and actions.
  3. Create a daily review checklist for messages and follow-ups.
  4. Explain how you will identify suspicious messages.
  5. Prepare a simple weekly email-management routine.

Final Checklist

  • Subject lines are clear.
  • Tone is polite and professional.
  • Recipients and attachments are checked.
  • Private information is protected.
  • Next steps and deadlines are clear.
